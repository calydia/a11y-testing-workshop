You can complete this path independently or use it as a next step after [Your first accessibility review](/learn/your-first-accessibility-review/). Split it across sessions if that helps you practise each technique with your chosen tools.

The demonstrations provide guided practice within the Testing methods. Image alternative text also has an independent Exercise where you investigate a deliberately created page. Revisit the methods while you practise, and record what your own screen-reader and browser combination does.

## Prepare your screen reader

Choose one screen reader that supports your operating system and browser. Use its authoritative help to find the commands you need; you do not need to memorize its complete command reference before continuing.

1. Record the screen reader, browser, operating system, and relevant versions you are using.
2. Learn how to start, pause or silence, and stop speech.
3. Practise moving sequentially through ordinary page content.
4. Learn how to navigate by headings and links and open element lists where your screen reader provides them.
5. Find out how your tool distinguishes browsing from direct interaction with controls, if it uses separate modes.
6. Record what is actually announced, along with the exposed state and interaction behavior.
7. Note installed voices, the initial page state, and other setup details when they may affect your results.

Commands, mode names, speech output, and supported behavior differ across screen readers, browsers, and operating systems. Test whether the information and interaction are useful rather than expecting one exact announcement.

## Where to go next

Use [Your first accessibility review](/learn/your-first-accessibility-review/) when you want to combine limited screen-reader checks with automated, keyboard, visual, zoom, and forms testing.

When you are ready to apply several techniques within one realistic scenario, continue to [Reviewing a course registration before launch](/journeys/reviewing-a-course-registration-before-launch/). That journey includes broader review work and does not assess every screen-reader topic in this path.

Additional Exercises may be added later for some of these methods. For now, use each method's demonstration to repeat the procedure and compare the behavior you observe with the explanation provided.
